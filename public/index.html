As I Was Bingeing Ahead Occasionally I Saw Brief Glimpses of Beauty

inspired by jonas mekas' 5-hour home-movie film and the culture of binge-watching, this work streams an endless sequence of random youtube videos— like channel-zapping through the intimate and mundane moments captured by strangers around the world.

all visitors worldwide see the same video at the same moment, watching together without knowing it.

videos sourced from youtube and invidious. each video plays for a fixed interval before cutting to the next.
